  ├─ [parallel] GPT-5.4-mini Vision     → foods + bbox_xyxy_norm + confidence

                  ↓
Confidence cascade (auto-routed)
  ├─ Tier 1 (avg ≥ 0.80)  → single-pass (~70 % of traffic)

                  ↓
